04Cloche

A silver serving dome — unmistakably the valet's world, and more elegant than a fork-and-knife cliché.
05Market Basket

A woven basket reads as provisioning the larder — warmer and more in-period than a shopping cart.
06Carriage Wheel

A spoked wheel nods to estate carriages while plainly meaning travel — the hub keeps the centre weighted.
07Estate Key

A classic key stands for the house and its upkeep. The bow carries the brass dot as a keyhole.
08Sundry Tag

The privacy promise made visible — a solid shackle for "nothing leaves your phone." The pin is the dot.
